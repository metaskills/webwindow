# WebWindow App Icon Prompts

## Variant 1 (`icon-1.png`)

Inside the squircle is a rich, deep twilight violet-to-indigo gradient backdrop with soft studio top-down lighting and subtle inner rim highlight. Floating at the center is a sleek, frameless translucent frosted glass browser pane with crisp specular edges and a soft drop shadow. At the very top edge of the floating glass pane, a ghostly, semi-transparent macOS title bar with three tiny glowing pastel traffic-light dots is gently fading into view, symbolizing a hidden title bar appearing on hover. Tactile 3D depth, Apple human interface design aesthetic, minimalist, ultra-clean, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermarks.
